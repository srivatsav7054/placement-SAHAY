# Placement SAHAY 🎯
### Your Companion for Placement Preparation

Placement SAHAY is a student-focused placement preparation platform designed to simplify the journey from resume building to job applications. It brings essential placement resources and tools together in one place, helping students prepare strategically, identify areas for improvement, and stay organized throughout the placement process.

## ✨ Features

- **Resume Builder** — Create and refine professional resumes.
- **AI-Powered Resume Analysis** — Analyze resumes, identify potential weaknesses, and receive actionable improvement suggestions.
- **ATS Compatibility Support** — Help students improve resume structure, relevant keywords, and readability for Applicant Tracking Systems.
- **Job Opportunities** — Explore relevant job openings and keep track of opportunities.
- **Application Tracking** — Organize job applications and monitor their progress.
- **Placement Preparation** — Structure preparation activities and track progress toward placement goals.
- **Notifications and Reminders** — Stay informed about important activities, deadlines, and opportunities.
- **Student Community** — Encourage collaboration and knowledge sharing among students.

*Feature availability depends on the current implementation of the platform.*

## 🎯 Objectives

- Simplify the placement preparation process for students.
- Help students create stronger, more effective resumes.
- Provide actionable insights to improve resume quality.
- Organize job opportunities and application progress.
- Encourage consistent, goal-oriented placement preparation.
- Bring important placement-related activities into a unified platform.
